WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This notice explains how information is handled when you visit evergreenconsultants.site and use the inquiry or client-onboarding request forms.

The current forms prepare a message in your own email application. They do not submit form data to a website server, create an online account, or store a password.

Information you choose to provide

Information entered into a form is placed into a pre-addressed email to Julius Nganga at bensontrucking247@gmail.com. You review that message and choose whether to send it. It may include your name, reply email, business name, location, requested support, and message.

How information is used

Information you send by email is used to respond to your inquiry, assess a potential engagement, discuss scope and next steps, and maintain related business correspondence. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in an inquiry.

Website storage and technology

The website does not currently provide hosted client accounts, online password storage, a server-side form database, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary technical request information to deliver and protect the website. Your email provider handles messages you choose to send.

Third-party services

Your email application, email provider, and website host process information as needed to provide their services. Their own privacy terms govern that processing.

Retention and requests

Messages may be retained as business correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a relationship, or meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. To ask about correspondence or request deletion where appropriate, email bensontrucking247@gmail.com.

Children

This website and its forms are intended for business users and are not designed for children.

Changes and contact

This notice may be updated when website features or information practices change. Privacy questions may be sent to Julius Nganga at bensontrucking247@gmail.com.
